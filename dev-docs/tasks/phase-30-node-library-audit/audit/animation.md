**Every node in the category is affected, at 2.50 per node** — the highest in the phase, ahead of
CustomCode's 2.00 and Navigation's 1.88. Two things drove it. The timer-leak shape sat in four
files because the node that got it right (`Delay`) is filed under a *different category*, so no
per-category read would ever have compared them. And the hyphen-splitting shape turned up
independently in `States` and in Component Utilities' `Component Object` on the same day, which
makes it a class rather than a typo: **the library encodes structured names into flat port strings
by concatenation and decodes them by `split('-')`, and nothing validates that the round trip is
lossless.** `Function`'s `'in' + n` and `String Mapper`'s numbered families are the same family of
mistake one step further out.
