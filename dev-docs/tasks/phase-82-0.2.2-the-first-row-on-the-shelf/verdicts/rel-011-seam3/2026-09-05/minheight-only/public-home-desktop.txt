Welcome

Stairs, windows and doors, out of oak, ash and elm.

Three of us work behind the station, in a shop that has been a joinery since 1911. We cut our own joints, we finish by hand, and we will tell you honestly when a repair is the better answer than a replacement.

Come and see the workshop — Thursdays, or by arrangement.
