## Register rules

- A row is closed by a commit hash and a one-line mechanism, or explicitly downgraded with a reason.
- **A decision is not a closure.** A row whose product question has been answered reads
  `**decided**, unowned` and stays in the table until something is built. `open` → `decided` →
  `closed`; skipping the middle state is how a decision becomes a thing everyone remembers agreeing
  and nobody remembers scheduling.
- **Rows stay in numeric order.** Not cosmetic: F3 spent two days below F15 and F8 two days between
  F5 and F6, and the phase HANDOVER's one-line summary of this register names 9 of the 15 rows —
  including neither of the two that were out of place.
- Anything discovered during AAQ work that doesn't belong to a numbered task lands here first —
  including new silent-discard mechanisms, which additionally get called out in the phase README if
  they change its counts.
- Never close a row on "couldn't reproduce" without recording the drive that failed to reproduce it
  (a driver that swallows a click failure reports the absence of a defect).
