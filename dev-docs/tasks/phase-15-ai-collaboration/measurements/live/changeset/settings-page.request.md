# What was asked for — /Pages/Account Settings

> An account settings page. A header with the member's name and avatar; a Profile section with their name, email and phone; a Notifications section with switches for email, push and the weekly digest; a Privacy section with a switch for a public profile and a link to the privacy policy; and at the bottom a danger zone with a Delete account button. Each section wants a heading and a divider above it, and there should be Save and Cancel buttons at the end.
