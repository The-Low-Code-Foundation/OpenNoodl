# Brief

## What this app is

An app for a ~12-person book club: members sign in with email/password, see the monthly book pick, and leave a short review with a 1-5 rating. No chat (WhatsApp covers that) and no voting on picks.

## Who uses it

About 12 book club members, all on phones, who already use WhatsApp for chat/discussion.

## Deliberately out of scope

- In-app chat or discussion (they use WhatsApp for that)
- Proposing books or voting on the next pick

_Agreed in the initial scoping conversation; the full record, including what was considered and rejected, is in `docs/decisions/000-initial-scope.md`._
