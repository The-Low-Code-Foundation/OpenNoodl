Every region is a scoped task

The mockup draws only what phase 68 scopes — each region below names the LB task that builds it. Two tasks have no honest place in a solo-pair screen and are listed as not pictured.

  • LB-001The tree. The sidebar: Program → Module → Thread, done-rollup, and who-sees-what (the hidden module).
  • LB-002Composer & thread. The centre pane: WYSIWYG posts with tables, replies, cross-references (the ↗ chip).
  • LB-003Notifications. The bell and unread dot; email digests and mutes behind it. Platform-wide, not LearnBook-only.
  • LB-004Groups — not pictured. A cohort here is UNI-005’s one roster, never a second group system. This screen shows a solo pair.
  • LB-005Visibility, progress, notes. The “Reveal to Maya” button, the progress card, and the coach-private session notes.
  • LB-006Assignments & live sessions. The amber validation state (UNI-006’s state machine, human grader) and the session card (a URL plus attendance — L6).
  • LB-007Rich media. The voice note and the sketch attachment; capped raw uploads, quotas per E4.
  • LB-008Templates. “Save as template” at the sidebar’s foot — the coach’s second cohort costs a clone, not a rebuild.
  • LB-009The sellable. The booking chip under the program title. Purchase-grants-access is blocked on ruling E2; until then the coach attaches the space by hand.
  • LB-010Safeguarding & exit. Report on every post’s ⋯ menu, “Export my space” in the coachee’s sidebar (E5), and the org-minor gate held shut (L5).

Deliberately not in the picture

  • No in-app video. The live session is a stored URL; the call runs on Meet, Zoom or Whereby (L6).
  • No real-time anything. No presence, no live cursors — the exchange is async-first.
  • No marketplace. No browse, ratings or take-rate; a program is reached from a coach’s profile and offer.
  • No search. The tree is small enough to browse in v1.
  • No minors. Org-minor accounts cannot enter coaching spaces until E3 is ruled (L5, default off).
  • Not built in NodeGX — yet. LearnBook ships on the platform stack (L1); the coachee frontend’s NodeGX rebuild is the pre-registered second act, once its missing pieces exist as published prefabs.
