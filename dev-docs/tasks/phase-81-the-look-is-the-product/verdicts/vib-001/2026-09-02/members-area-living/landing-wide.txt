About us
St Anywhere is a parish congregation of about ninety people. We meet on Sunday mornings, run the Tuesday lunch club, keep the churchyard, and put on a summer fete that has not been rained off since 2019. Everyone is welcome at anything on the diary.
Membership is by request: ask to join and a moderator will approve you.
